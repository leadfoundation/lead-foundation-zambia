Global Warming – Climate Change Awareness Through Music

This video features one of our dedicated peer educators, Nambale Bwale, performing her song titled "Global Warming" to raise awareness on climate change. Through her powerful message, she highlights the growing impact of climate change on communities, particularly affecting the lives of children. Her initiative reflects the passion and commitment of young leaders in driving meaningful change. We commend her efforts and celebrate her contribution to promoting environmental awareness.
