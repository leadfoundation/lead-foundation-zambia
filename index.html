About Us

Our mission is to create meaningful change by spreading ideas, imparting skills, and fostering community participation. Lead Foundation Zambia empowers youth and women with tools, skills and legal knowledge to actively participate in governance and leadership. We promote human rights, climate resilience and sustainable development for current and future generations.
